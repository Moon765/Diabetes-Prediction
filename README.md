<h2>📌 Project Overview</h2>
<p>
This project applies <b>machine learning models</b> to predict the likelihood of diabetes based on patient health data.  
It follows a full ML pipeline: <b>data cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis, training, hyperparameter tuning, and evaluation</b>.
</p>

---

<h2>⚡ Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>🧹 <b>Data Preprocessing</b> – replaced invalid zeros with NaN & imputed values</li>
  <li>📊 <b>Exploratory Data Analysis</b> – histograms & correlation plots</li>
  <li>🤖 <b>Machine Learning Models</b> – Logistic Regression, KNN, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Neural Networks</li>
  <li>🎯 <b>Model Tuning</b> – GridSearchCV with cross-validation</li>
  <li>📈 <b>Evaluation</b> – Accuracy & Confusion Matrices</li>
</ul>

<h2>🚀 Workflow</h2>
<ol>
  <li>Load dataset & inspect for missing/invalid values</li>
  <li>Replace zeros with NaN → Impute using mean/median</li>
  <li>EDA → histograms, correlation heatmap</li>
  <li>Split into training & testing sets</li>
  <li>Train models (Logistic Regression, KNN, Decision Tree, Random Forest, NN)</li>
  <li>Tune hyperparameters with GridSearchCV</li>
  <li>Evaluate & compare results</li>
</ol>

---

<h2>📊 Results</h2>
<ul>
  <li>✅ Models classified diabetic vs non-diabetic cases effectively</li>
  <li>📈 Ensemble models (Random Forest) generally achieved better performance</li>
  <li>⚠️ Dataset imbalances and preprocessing had strong impact on accuracy</li>
</ul>
